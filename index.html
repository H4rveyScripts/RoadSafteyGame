Road Safety Game — Australia

Drive on the left. Speed in km/h. Follow signs, stop at crossings, watch school zones.

Press SPACE to start — use ← → or A/D to steer, S to brake

How to play

  • Drive on the left-hand side of the road.
  • Obey speed limits (km/h). School zones and roadworks reduce limits.
  • Stop for pedestrians at zebra crossings and at red lights.
  • Avoid collisions — they cost lives and points.
  • Australian signs are used for teaching — look out for "School Zone 40", "Give Way", and speed signs.
